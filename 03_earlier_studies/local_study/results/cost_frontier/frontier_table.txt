INFORMATION-COST FRONTIER: Blind at Scale, 99-instance synthetic study
======================================================================
Source: results/cost_frontier/frontier.csv (generated by
derive_cost_frontier.py from results/summary.json, results/eqinfo/,
results/ablation/ and on-disk artifact sizes). Bytes are means across
the 9 datasets at 10M fact rows unless noted. Q-error = median
max(pred/gt, gt/pred) over instances. All frontier arms below have
recall 1.000 (60/60 FAIL detected, 0 FP), so verdict accuracy does not
discriminate; Q-error is the error axis. Pareto = no other point has
both fewer bytes AND lower median Q-error.

Dominance notes (all on the pooled median-Q axis):
- A8_k256 is dominated by A8_k64 (fewer bytes AND lower Q; the k axis
  is NOT monotone in pooled median Q: k64 1.000043 < k256 1.000065,
  and the same ordering holds on J-family Q, 1.038909 vs 1.042788).
- A5 is dominated by A8_k1024: identical synopsis bytes, worse Q
  (testbed multinomial sampling noise; A5 == A8 on verdicts).
- A7 is dominated by A8_k16 (13x fewer bytes, lower Q) and A7X by
  A8_k64 (9x fewer bytes, lower Q).
- A4 is dominated by every A8 rung: most bytes, worst Q among the
  recall-1.0 arms.
- CLASSICAL_k1024 (A9) is dominated by A8_k64 (6,744 B / 1.000043)
  and by A7X_k1024 (58,471 B / 1.000487): both fewer bytes AND lower
  median Q. A9 matches A5 on verdicts and median Q (1.001550 vs
  1.001465) at 0.64x the synopsis bytes, but it is not a frontier
  point: the frontier is entirely A8 depth rungs.
- A1/A2/A3 are NOT frontier points: side-information bytes NOT
  MEASURED (no testbed artifact exists on disk) and recall < 1.0
  (0.767 / 0.517 / 0.417). See cost_access_table.csv.

Depth matters most on join pipelines: J-family median Q falls
1.061 (k16) -> 1.039 (k64) -> 1.009 (k1024); the pooled median hides
this because G/D/W families saturate at Q = 1.000 at every rung.
